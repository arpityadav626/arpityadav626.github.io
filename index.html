Smart Rider: Connected Safety Helmet & Ignition Interlock

Problem Statement

Two-wheeler riders represent a disproportionate share of road fatalities due to riding without helmets, alcohol impairment, and delayed post-crash rescue dispatch. Traditional helmets act as passive gear without active prevention or emergency alerting.

My Contribution & Role

As a core member of Team Smart Rider, I contributed to the technical concept development, dual-unit system partitioning, sensor evaluation (FSR, MQ-3 alcohol, MPU6050 accelerometer/gyro), and local fail-safe interlock evaluation logic.

Architecture & Signal Flow

[HELMET UNIT: ESP32]
├── FSR (Helmet Worn Check)
├── MQ-3 (Alcohol Threshold)
└── MPU6050 (Impact & Tilt)
│ (433 MHz RF Packet)
▼
[BIKE CONTROLLER: ESP32]
├── Safe Pre-Checks ──► Ignition Interlock Relay
└── Impact Detected ──► Buzzer Alert Window ──► GPS + Cellular Emergency SMS

Engineering Decisions

  • Edge-First Processing: Core start interlock decisions run locally on ESP32 microcontrollers—zero dependency on internet connectivity for basic rider safety.
  • False-Alert Reset Window: Potholes or dropped helmets trigger an audible buzzer window allowing riders to cancel false emergency dispatches before cellular SMS alerts fire.
Verification Status: Concept architecture & test bench evaluated at SIH 2026 Internal Hackathon (Axis Institute of Technology & Management).

Problem Statement

Modern cybercrime investigations handle disparate digital logs (server CSVs, WhatsApp messages, SMTP headers, cellular tower CDRs). In court, standard Large Language Models hallucinate timestamps and invent citations, breaching evidentiary admissibility under Indian and international law.

My Contribution & Role

Served as Project Lead and System Architect for the hackathon project. I engineered the dual-hash Merkle tree engine, Allen's interval algebra implementation, zero-hallucination mechanical verifier, and the automated Section 63(4) BSA statutory certificate generator.

Core Algorithmic Engines

  • Dual-Hash & Merkle Engine: Computes concurrent NIST FIPS 180-4 SHA-256 and FIPS 202 SHA3-256 digests over 64KB streams. Enforces 1-bit tamper interception via ForensicIntegrityViolationError.
  • Allen's Temporal Interval Algebra: Implements James Allen's 13 interval relations with dual-clock NTP drift calibration ([t_min, t_max]).

  • Mechanical Citation Gate: Grounds every finding in exact byte spans (byte_start, byte_end). Unsubstantiated prompts are rejected with a strict 0.0% Grounding Score.

CASE 03 // EMBEDDED & 3D WEB Physical Hardware + Web Twin

SAFETY-FI 96X: Industrial-Grade Kitchen IoT Safety Platform

Problem Statement

Undetected LPG gas leaks and runaway domestic fires cause devastating kitchen explosions. Typical domestic alarms provide passive beeps without automatic physical containment (ventilation fan actuation, fire suppression, or remote telemetry).

My Contribution & Role

As sole builder, I engineered the complete working physical model: assembled the Arduino Uno circuit with isolated 5V relays, wrote non-blocking C++ firmware (smart_kitchen.ino), developed the asynchronous Python serial telemetry server (app.py), and created the Three.js 3D digital twin web dashboard.

Firmware & Backend Engineering

  • Instant 5-Second One-Shot Flame Timer: Optical IR flame detection activates the water pump relay and acoustic alarm for exactly 5,000 ms before deterministic auto shut-off.
  • Anti-Loop Lockout (flameMustClear): Prevents infinite alarm cycling during sensor exposure by requiring the flame to clear before re-arming the actuator trigger.
  • Calibrated Gas Threshold (300 ADC): Tuned the MQ-2 hydrocarbon trigger to 300 ADC units, eliminating ambient indoor drift while reacting instantly to butane/LPG concentrations.
  • Reentrant Lock Concurrency (threading.RLock): Eliminated server thread starvation in Python, allowing continuous 9600-Baud USB serial polling and REST API requests concurrently.
  • Three.js 3D Digital Twin: Client-side 3D cooktop rendering with dynamic particle fluids, electric blue (#38BDF8) gas alert lighting, and live hardware register telemetry.
Deployment Architecture: Physical model operates with Arduino Uno & local Python telemetry server. The GitHub Pages deployment provides an interactive Three.js 3D digital twin and hybrid simulation engine.
